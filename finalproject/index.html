The story behind the frames

Frames of Kampala is a personal documentary portfolio focused on honest moments: a market before noon, a quiet lakeshore, a maker at work, and neighbors sharing a street.

Observe

I begin by listening to the pace and texture of a place rather than arriving with a fixed picture.

Connect

Trust and respect guide each portrait and every interaction with a community.

Preserve

The finished frame keeps an ordinary moment visible for longer than a passing glance.
