Community access

NP Membership

For registered non-profit organizations supporting Kampala communities. There is no membership fee.

  • Chamber community and member directory listing
  • Invitations to selected networking events
  • Access to shared community and partnership opportunities

Start connecting

Bronze Membership

For growing businesses ready to build local relationships. Sample annual dues: UGX 150,000.

  • All NP community access benefits
  • Standard business listing in the member directory
  • Monthly networking event invitations
  • Member-rate access to introductory training

Build your reach

Silver Membership

For established organizations looking to increase visibility and opportunity. Sample annual dues: UGX 350,000.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Priority directory placement
  • Discounted tickets for selected chamber events
  • Quarterly member spotlight eligibility
  • One included seat at selected business training

Lead the community

Gold Membership

For organizations seeking premium visibility and deeper chamber participation. Sample annual dues: UGX 700,000.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Featured directory placement and homepage spotlight eligibility
  • Reserved invitations to leadership and partnership forums
  • Priority access and additional discounts for chamber events
  • Additional training seats and promotional opportunities
